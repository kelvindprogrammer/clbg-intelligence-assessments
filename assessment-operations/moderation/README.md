# Moderation and Quality Assurance

Moderation keeps outcomes **consistent across assessors and cohorts**. It complements the [evaluation framework](../../docs/evaluation-framework.md).

## 1. Calibration session (before each cohort)

1. The committee chooses 2–3 calibration exemplars per subtrack from the assessor kit (`calibration/`). These are anonymised past submissions or constructed exemplars.
2. Each assessor scores them **independently**, using the rubric and the marking guide.
3. In the session, compare scores dimension by dimension and discuss every difference larger than 5 points on a dimension.
4. Record agreed interpretations in the kit's `calibration/decisions.md`. They become guidance for the cohort.
5. New assessors mark live work only after scoring within ±6 total of the agreed score on two exemplars.

## 2. Double marking

| Trigger | Procedure |
|---|---|
| Tier 3, capstone, Signature Challenge | Always double-marked, with two assessors at the defence. |
| Band boundary (total within ±3 of 50, 65 or 80) | A second assessor marks blind. |
| Random sample (10% of everything else) | A second assessor marks blind. |
| Appeal | A different assessor marks blind (see [assessment policy §9](../../docs/assessment-policy.md#9-disputes-and-appeals)). |

**Resolution.** If the totals differ by fewer than 10, use the mean (rounded), and the outcome follows the mean. If they differ by 10 or more, the assessors discuss. If they still can't agree, a third assessor marks and the median is used.

## 3. End-of-cohort moderation meeting

Agenda:
1. **Distribution review.** The score distribution per assessment and per assessor, and pass rates per tier and subtrack.
2. **Inter-rater reliability.** The mean absolute difference per dimension on double-marked work. The target is ≤ 1.5 per dimension and ≤ 6 total.
3. **Outliers.** Any assessor whose mean deviates from the panel mean by more than 8 points across 5 or more submissions should be discussed supportively, followed by recalibration.
4. **Assessment health.** Pass rates below 20% or above 95% (minimum 10 attempts), recurring ambiguities, broken resources, member feedback.
5. **Actions.** Rubric clarifications, marking-guide updates, version bumps and retirements, logged in the moderation record.

## 4. Moderation record

Keep it privately in the assessor kit (`moderation/records/<cohort>.md`) with: attendance, the statistics, decisions, actions, owners and due dates. Never include member names in the public repository.
