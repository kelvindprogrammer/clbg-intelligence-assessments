# Code of Conduct

CLBG is a community of builders who learn by doing, and who learn from each other. We want everyone to be able to take part in assessment, review and contribution without harassment or discrimination, whatever their background, identity, level of experience, faculty or year of study.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive, in reviews, feedback, issues and defences alike.
- Critique work, not people. Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Help newcomers. Everyone was a beginner in some subtrack once.
- Respect the integrity of assessments. Don't share confidential materials or other members' solutions.
- Respect privacy. Never post other people's personal data or assessment outcomes.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory jokes or language, or personal attacks.
- Mocking someone's skill level, accent, language or background, especially during a technical defence.
- Pressuring others to share solutions, or to cover for integrity breaches.
- Publishing other people's private information.

## Assessor conduct

Assessors hold a position of trust. They must declare conflicts of interest, keep evaluations evidence-based, and treat members with patience and dignity. That matters most when work falls short.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the Intelligence Track lead, or to any member of the Assessment Committee who is not involved. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly. Responses range from a private conversation to removal from CLBG activities, in proportion to the behaviour.
