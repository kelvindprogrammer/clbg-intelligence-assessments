# Vision and Objectives

## 1. Vision

Every CLBG member who joins the Intelligence Track can **find out honestly what they can do, grow through projects worth doing, and show credible evidence of it**, in Data Science, ML Engineering and AI Engineering, whatever laptop they own.

## 2. Objectives

| # | Objective | How the ecosystem delivers it |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | **Place members fairly and accurately**, per subtrack | Evidence-based placement with rubric, gates and defence ([placement framework](placement-framework.md)) |
| O2 | **Develop real competence**, not test-taking | Assessments that teach something valuable, each answering eight pedagogical questions ([authoring guide](contributing/AUTHORING_GUIDE.md#1-the-design-test)) |
| O3 | **Keep standards consistent** across assessors and cohorts | Analytic rubrics, calibration, double marking and moderation ([evaluation framework](evaluation-framework.md)) |
| O4 | **Stay current with industry** | Calibration against current curricula, certifications and practice ([Research and Standards Report](research/RESEARCH_AND_STANDARDS_REPORT.md)) |
| O5 | **Be accessible** | CPU first, free tools, no required paid APIs ([ADR-012](research/architecture-decisions.md#adr-012-accessibility)) |
| O6 | **Model responsible practice** | NDPA 2023-aware data handling, security and responsible AI in every assessment |
| O7 | **Be maintainable by future executives** | Validated specifications, generated catalogue, documented lifecycle ([maintenance guide](contributing/maintenance.md)) |

## 3. The pathway: Research → Build → Demonstrate → Evaluate → Grow

| Stage | What the member does | What CLBG provides |
|---|---|---|
| **Research** | Reads the brief, investigates the problem, studies the methods and data | A realistic problem statement, prerequisites and resources (sections B and E) |
| **Build** | Implements, analyses or designs, using tools openly and declaring their use | Starters without solutions, contract tests, data, clear requirements (sections D, F, K) |
| **Demonstrate** | Submits reproducible work and defends it live: tracing, explaining, modifying | Submission guidelines and a public [defence guide](../assessment-operations/oral-defense/technical-defence-guide.md) |
| **Evaluate** | Receives an outcome from hidden tests, rubric scoring, gates and the defence | Trained assessors, calibration and moderation |
| **Grow** | Acts on specific feedback, revises or tries another assessment, moves up a tier | Feedback within 10 working days, revision and retry routes, no permanent labels |

## 4. Success measures

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Inter-rater agreement on double-marked work | Mean absolute difference ≤ 1.5 points per dimension and ≤ 6 points total |
| Feedback turnaround | ≥ 90% within 10 working days |
| Assessment health | Pass rate between 20% and 95% over ≥ 10 attempts for each assessment; outliers reviewed |
| Coverage | Planned unit-tier cells with at least one `reviewed` assessment, tracked in the generated coverage matrix |
| Accessibility | 100% of assessments completable on CPU or a free tier with no required paid API |
| Integrity | Every placement above Tier 1 supported by a recorded defence |
| Member growth | Share of members with an R or N outcome who later achieve M or M− in the same unit |

## 5. Non-goals

- **Not a ranking of people.** Tiers describe demonstrated competence in one subtrack. Only Signature Challenges have a competitive element, under their own protocol.
- **Not a course or a credential.** The ecosystem assesses and develops; it does not award academic credit or replace university study.
- **Not a certification substitute.** Industry certifications are used for calibration only.
- **Not AI-tool policing.** Integrity rests on verification, never on detectors.
- **Not dependent on paid infrastructure** or any single vendor.
- **Not a store of personal data.** Placement records hold the minimum required, privately ([assessment policy §10](assessment-policy.md#10-records-and-privacy)).
