# Assessment Policy

This policy governs how CLBG Intelligence Track placement assessments are offered, attempted, submitted, evaluated and recorded. It applies to every subtrack and tier.

## 1. Placement window

This assessment runs **9–13 November 2026**. It opens today and closes Friday. Results are announced the following week.

## 2. Purpose of assessment

Assessments exist to **evidence competence fairly** and **inform placement and team formation**. They are not competitions, and they don't rank members against each other.

## 3. Eligibility and choice

- Each member chooses **one subtrack, one tier, and one unit/assessment** for this window. That single choice is the member's attempt.
- **Prerequisites** are listed in each assessment. They are advisory, not gates.

## 4. The standard assessment specification

Every assessment follows the standard format (sections A–K plus Pedagogical Design). An assessment can only be attempted when its status is `reviewed` or `approved`.

## 5. Registering an attempt

1. Open an **Assessment attempt** issue in this repository using its template. Record the assessment ID and its version.
2. An assessor is assigned.
3. The assessment **version** at registration is the one assessed.

## 6. Collaboration

Each assessment's `collaboration` field (individual, pair or team) defines what is permitted:

| Mode | Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | Discussing general techniques, concepts and documentation; asking mentors conceptual questions. | Sharing code, notebooks, results, report text or rules specific to the assessment. |
| **Pair / team** | Full collaboration within the declared group. | Undeclared contributors. Each member must be able to defend **every** part of the work, and contribution statements are required. |

## 7. Deadlines

All submissions are due by **Friday 13 November 2026**. There is no extension or late-work allowance within this window; members needing adjustments should contact the committee directly.

## 8. Evaluation

Evaluation follows the [evaluation framework](evaluation-framework.md) and the [placement framework](placement-framework.md):
- rubric scoring by a trained assessor using the public rubric and the confidential marking guide;
- gates (integrity, reproducibility, defence, plus any assessment-specific gate);
- a technical defence for every outcome above N.

Results are announced the week of **16 November 2026**.

## 9. Disputes and appeals

A member who believes their result contains a procedural error or a factual error in assessing the work may ask the committee to review it within 7 days of the result. A different assessor reviews the contested points.

## 10. Records and privacy

- Placement records contain: name, GitHub username, assessment IDs and versions, outcomes, factor judgements, assessor names and dates. **No other personal data is kept.**
- Records are visible only to the member, the assessors involved and the committee. They are stored privately, never in the public repository.
- Members can request a copy or correction of their record at any time. This follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 principles of purpose limitation, minimisation and storage limitation.

## 11. Integrity

See [assessment integrity](assessment-integrity.md). Suspected breaches are handled under that policy, never informally.

## 12. Accessibility and reasonable adjustments

Members with disabilities or other circumstances that affect assessment may ask for adjustments, such as a written instead of an oral defence component. Adjustments change *how* competence is shown, never *what* competence is required.
