# DS-T1-027 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know exactly how you will be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration notes, but it never adds criteria that are not listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 10 | The report shows why each of the four promises matters to the revenue office and how a failure would show up in the monthly total. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 20 | Public and hidden tests pass; counts equal ground truth; crash-and-retry works; invariants hold. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 15 | Small, readable functions; a clear transaction boundary; no copy-paste; standard library only. |
| 4 | Methodology and evidence | 15 | Rules are derived from inspecting the files; the quarantine breakdown is generated by code. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 15 | At least 8 meaningful member tests, including a crash test and a conflict test. |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 10 | Clean-environment run works; the summary regenerates from the code. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 10 | Honest account of what the loader does not handle (concurrency, schema change, clock). |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | A revenue officer can read the load summary and trust it. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90–100% | Exceeds the requirement with insight, for example by quantifying a risk or testing an assumption unprompted. |
| **Proficient** | 70–89% | Fully meets the requirement with minor gaps that do not affect trustworthiness. |
| **Developing** | 50–69% | Partly meets the requirement: some choices unjustified or untested. |
| **Beginning** | 0–49% | Missing, incorrect or unexplained. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** the AI-use declaration in `REPORT.md` is present and consistent with what the defence shows.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns your tests from a clean environment and gets your reported results.
3. **Defence:** you trace assessor-chosen cases and handle the unseen modification with sound reasoning.
4. **Privacy and safety:** no personal data in the report; any safeguards required in §D.3 are present.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Rows silently dropped or overwritten | Up to −10 from dimension 2 |
| Idempotency keyed on file name only | Up to −6 from dimension 2 |
| Report numbers not regenerated by the code | Up to −5 from dimension 6 |
