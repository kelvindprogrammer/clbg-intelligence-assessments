# DS-T1-010 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know exactly how you will be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide, but it never adds criteria that are not listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 10 | You explain why each naive shortcut (summing, ignoring coverage, i.i.d. days) biases the answer, and in which direction. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 25 | Daily supply matches the minute-grid oracle on re-generated logs; issue counts are exact; the bootstrap follows the specification; the planted misbanded feeders and collapse dates are found. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 15 | A clean, efficient interval union; no mutation; a readable separation between parsing, conversion and uncertainty; the CLI finishes in under 2 minutes. |
| 4 | Exploratory methodology | 10 | A heat map or equivalent that exposes gaps and collapse days; the cluster versus i.i.d. comparison; evidence for each answer. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 15 | At least 10 member tests, including at least 4 hand-worked interval cases (multi-day event, exactly-midnight boundary, duplicate-closing open event, export-time end). |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 10 | Every report number is regenerated by code; the run instructions work first time. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 10 | Inconclusive feeders are named and explained; the open-event rule is described as a convention; unlogged interruptions are acknowledged. |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | A compliance manager could act on the report: answers first, intervals shown, no unexplained jargon. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90–100% | Exceeds the requirement with insight, e.g. a block-length sensitivity analysis or a customer-impact estimate with an interval. |
| **Proficient** | 70–89% | Fully meets the requirement with minor gaps. |
| **Developing** | 50–69% | Partly meets it, e.g. correct conversion but i.i.d. uncertainty, or gaps treated as full supply. |
| **Beginning** | 0–49% | Durations summed, coverage ignored, or answers without evidence. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** `AI_USE_AND_SOURCES.md` is present and consistent with the defence.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns the CLI and exploration code from a clean environment and gets the submitted outputs.
3. **Defence:** the member computes daily supply for three hand-drawn intervals and locates each step in their code.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| A data file was modified (checksum mismatch) | Gate fail until corrected |
| Misbanded feeders or collapse dates hard-coded | Up to −15 from dimension 2 |
| Report numbers not regenerated by code | Up to −5 from dimension 6 |
