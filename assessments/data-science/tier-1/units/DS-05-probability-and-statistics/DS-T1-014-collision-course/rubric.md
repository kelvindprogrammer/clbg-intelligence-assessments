# DS-T1-014 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know exactly how you'll be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration examples, but it never adds criteria that aren't listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 10 | You explain the difference between "this booking repeats" and "some pair repeats", and why references are bearer credentials whose length is also a security parameter. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 20 | Exact probabilities accurate where naive code fails; correct derivations of E[pairs] and Σpᵢ²; exact boundary values for length and capacity; a correct exact-Poisson interval for N. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 10 | Stable numerics (`log1p`/`expm1` or equivalent); vectorised simulation; clear separation of theory and evidence; input validation as specified. |
| 4 | Methodology: investigation and evidence | 20 | Per-day and pooled evidence; the design's risk separated from the implementation defect; the space estimate with an interval; the frequency check used and its blind spot explained; simulation verifying every formula with a stated tolerance. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 15 | At least 10 member tests, including enumeration for a tiny case and a test that catches naive floating point. |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 10 | One script regenerates every number and figure; seeds stated; the CLI output matches the report. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 10 | What two days at one depot can and cannot show; that the mechanism (a 24-bit state, or something else) is a hypothesis; trade-offs among the three designs. |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | A short answer the head of digital products can act on, with redacted examples only. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90–100% | Correct and insightful: for example, identifying the likely bit width with a likelihood comparison, or a first-repeat estimator cross-check. |
| **Proficient** | 70–89% | All requirements met, with a sound investigation and recommendation and minor gaps in explanation. |
| **Developing** | 50–69% | Formulas mostly right, but the investigation conflates the design's risk with the defect, omits intervals, or recommends a length without the guessing requirement. |
| **Beginning** | 0–49% | P ≈ n/N reasoning, naive numerics that fail, or no evidence-based conclusion about the generator. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** `AI_USE_AND_SOURCES.md` is present and consistent with what the defence shows.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns the investigation from a clean environment and gets the reported numbers.
3. **Defence:** the member derives E[pairs] and handles the unseen modification with sound reasoning.
4. **Credential hygiene:** no more than three redacted references from the log appear in the report.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| The reference log was modified (checksum mismatch) | Gate fail until corrected |
| Report states the design risk as about n/N | Up to −5 from dimension 1 |
| Report numbers not regenerated by the code | Up to −5 from dimension 6 |
