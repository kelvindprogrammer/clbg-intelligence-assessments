# DS-T1-025 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know exactly how you will be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration notes, but it never adds criteria that are not listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 10 | The report shows why gaps are unknown rather than zero and why the scale must be causal; choices are justified by consequences. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 20 | Public and hidden tests pass; leakage test passes; hidden baseline choice is at least 7 of 8. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 15 | Small clear functions, no mutation, no copy-paste, deterministic. |
| 4 | Methodology and evidence | 15 | The defect inventory (gaps, sentinels, duplicates) is quantified by code and every rule traces to it. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 15 | At least 8 meaningful member tests, including edge cases found in the data. |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 10 | A clean-environment run works first time; the report's numbers regenerate from the code. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 10 | Honest account of what 8 synthetic agents and a short backtest can and cannot show. |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | Concise, readable report; a planner could act on the table. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90–100% | Exceeds the requirement with insight, for example by quantifying a risk or testing an assumption unprompted. |
| **Proficient** | 70–89% | Fully meets the requirement with minor gaps that do not affect trustworthiness. |
| **Developing** | 50–69% | Partly meets the requirement: some choices unjustified or untested. |
| **Beginning** | 0–49% | Missing, incorrect or unexplained. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** the AI-use declaration in `REPORT.md` is present and consistent with what the defence shows.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns your tests from a clean environment and gets your reported results.
3. **Defence:** you trace assessor-chosen cases and handle the unseen modification with sound reasoning.
4. **Privacy and safety:** no personal data in the report; any safeguards required in §D.3 are present.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Unknown days turned into zeros | Up to −8 from dimension 2 |
| Metric scale computed from the whole series (leakage) | Up to −8 from dimension 2 |
| Report numbers not regenerated by the code | Up to −5 from dimension 6 |
