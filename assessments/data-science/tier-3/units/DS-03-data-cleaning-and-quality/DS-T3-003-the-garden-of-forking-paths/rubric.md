# DS-T3-003 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know exactly how you'll be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration exemplars, but it never adds criteria that aren't listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem framing and estimand | 15 | A precise estimand; explicit recognition that some forks (closures, treatment definition, outcome scale) change *what* is estimated; an analysis organised accordingly. |
| 2 | Technical correctness and reproducibility | 15 | Public and hidden tests pass; universes reproduce exactly; `data_dir` is respected; the estimator and clustered SEs are correct (checked on a constructed panel). |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 10 | Cleaning steps are separate tested functions; one-command regeneration; no hidden state between universes. |
| 4 | Research methodology | 20 | Six or more decisions with defensible options grounded in the data and the literature; argued exclusions; a space neither padded nor narrowed; revisions documented in the commit history. |
| 5 | Analysis, inference and ablation | 15 | Specification curves per comparable estimand; quantitative attribution to decisions; a robustness-inference method or an argued alternative; at least one ablation. |
| 6 | Documentation | 5 | The report, spec and code agree; figures regenerate. |
| 7 | Validity, critical thinking and limitations | 15 | Design threats (parallel trends, selection through closure, measurement error) and multiverse threats (the space is a choice); honest negative or mixed results. |
| 8 | Communication | 5 | A funder paragraph that is honest and usable; a data-collection note that follows from the attribution. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90–100% | Research-grade. The member identifies the estimand-changing forks and reorganises the analysis around them, quantifies attribution with interactions, and performs a joint robustness test. |
| **Proficient** | 70–89% | Sound and complete, with minor gaps. For example, attribution only by main effects, or a light treatment of multiverse threats. |
| **Developing** | 50–69% | A working multiverse, but estimates on different scales pooled, weak rationales, or a "share significant" summary as the only analysis. |
| **Beginning** | 0–49% | Few or indefensible forks, non-reproducible results, or conclusions not supported by the multiverse. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** `AI_USE_AND_SOURCES.md` is present and consistent with the defence.
2. **Reproducibility:** the multiverse regenerates from a clean environment with the documented command.
3. **Defence:** the member defends two decisions and predicts the effect of an unseen fork.
4. **Research integrity:** no evidence that the decision space was tuned to produce a desired conclusion without disclosure.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Results pooled across outcome scales in a headline robustness statistic without justification | Up to −8 from dimension 5 |
| `run_universe` ignores `data_dir` | Up to −10 from dimension 2 |
| Report figures not regenerated by the analysis code | Up to −5 from dimension 6 |
