# DS-T3-005 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know how you will be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration notes, but it never adds criteria that are not listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 15 | Explains rate versus mix and the artefact in terms a commissioner would grasp, with numbers from the data. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 20 | Public and hidden tests pass: exact decomposition, reversal logic, break detection and correction on unseen seeds. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 10 | Vectorised pandas, clear functions, no hard-coded operators, routes or dates. |
| 4 | Methodology and exploration discipline | 20 | The ledger shows real hypotheses, falsification attempts and at least two refuted ideas; the break-detection method is justified against alternatives. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 10 | Hand-checkable unit cases for the decomposition and break detection; sensitivity of the corrected headline to method choices. |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 5 | Every number and figure regenerates from the notebook or script. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 15 | States what the exploration cannot establish (causal effect of the timetable), uncertainty on the shift, and what data would settle it. |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | Finding-titled figures, a decision-ready paragraph, readable tables. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90-100% | Exceeds the requirement with insight, for example by quantifying uncertainty or testing an assumption nobody asked about. |
| **Proficient** | 70-89% | Fully meets the requirement with minor gaps that do not affect trustworthiness. |
| **Developing** | 50-69% | Partly meets the requirement; some claims are unjustified or untested. |
| **Beginning** | 0-49% | Missing, incorrect or unexplained. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** `AI_USE_AND_SOURCES.md` is present and consistent with what the defence shows.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns your work from a clean environment and obtains the submitted results.
3. **Defence:** you trace assessor-chosen results and handle an unseen modification with sound reasoning.
4. **Privacy and ethics:** no real personal data is introduced; any limits stated in §D are respected.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Operator, route or date hard-coded in the detection or correction | Up to -10 from dimension 2 |
| Ledger with no refuted hypotheses or fabricated evidence references | Up to -8 from dimension 4 |
| Report numbers not regenerated by the notebook or script | Up to -5 from dimension 6 |
