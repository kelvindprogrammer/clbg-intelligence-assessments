# DS-T2-009 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know exactly how you'll be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration examples, but it never adds criteria that aren't listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 10 | You explain why tiny schools dominate both ends of the raw table, what "long-run rate" means, and why the choice of shrinkage target is a substantive decision, not a default. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 15 | Correct marginal likelihood and posterior; hidden-truth MSE at most 70% of raw; no stratum bias beyond 0.045; calibrated intervals; correct handling of edge cases. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 15 | Estimators behind one interface; the evaluation harness independent of the estimator; no duplicated fitting code; robust to zero and all-pass schools, missing years and row order. |
| 4 | Methodology: model choice and evaluation design | 20 | Exploration that motivates the prior; at least three estimators compared fairly; a held-out design that corrects for noise in the held-out rates; uncertainty reported for differences; a sensitivity analysis of the lists. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 15 | At least 12 member tests, including a hand-computed posterior and recovery of known hyperparameters from simulated data; coverage reported by school size. |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 10 | One command regenerates outputs and figures; seeds stated; the README run instructions work. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 10 | Names the assumptions (exchangeability within strata, stable rates), the groups most affected, what three years cannot tell you, and an honest note on interval calibration for large schools. |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | A Commissioner's brief that is accurate, plain and fair, with disclosure control for small cohorts. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90–100% | Covariate-aware shrinkage justified by exploration, a noise-corrected evaluation with intervals on the differences, calibration fixed or explained by size, and a loss-based selection rule. |
| **Proficient** | 70–89% | Meets all criteria with a sound comparison. Minor gaps, for example calibration reported but not explained. |
| **Developing** | 50–69% | A working empirical Bayes estimator, but shrinkage to one grand mean without checking for structure, an evaluation that ignores held-out noise, or no uncertainty on the comparison. |
| **Beginning** | 0–49% | Raw ranking, ad-hoc minimum-size filters presented as estimation, or an estimator that is not demonstrably better than raw. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** `AI_USE_AND_SOURCES.md` is present and consistent with what the defence shows.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns the study from a clean environment and gets the submitted outputs.
3. **Defence:** the member derives the shrinkage weight and handles the unseen modification with sound reasoning.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Data files modified (checksum mismatch) | Gate fail until corrected |
| Evaluation uses information from the held-out year to fit the estimator | Up to −10 from dimension 4 |
| Report numbers not regenerated by the code | Up to −5 from dimension 6 |
