# DS-T2-010 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know exactly how you'll be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration examples, but it never adds criteria that aren't listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 10 | You explain return periods correctly ("probability 1/T each year"), why the shape parameter dominates the 100-year uncertainty, and why a design life changes the question. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 15 | Correct MLE fits and sign convention; correct derivations of both return-level formulas; correct declustering; an interval method that is calibrated in simulation. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 10 | Library conventions wrapped and tested; fitting robust to starting values; no duplicated GEV/GPD code; contract functions pure. |
| 4 | Methodology: choices, diagnostics and simulation checks | 25 | An evidence-based coverage rule; threshold stability and mean-residual-life analysis; diagnostics for both methods; a simulation study of bias, RMSE and coverage; a non-stationary model with a test **and** its power. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 10 | At least 10 member tests, including the sign flip, the Gumbel limit and declustering edge cases. |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 10 | One command regenerates all numbers and figures; seeds stated; for the optional real-data path, a download script, version and access date. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 15 | Honest about extrapolation; correct reasoning about a non-significant trend over 40 years; the limitations of daily data and a single gauge (and of gridded reanalysis, if used); who bears which risk. |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | A design note an engineer can use: numbers, ranges, assumptions and an argued margin. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90–100% | Calibrated intervals demonstrated by simulation; GEV and POT reconciled; trend analysis with power and a risk-based design argument; the real-data critique (optional) insightful. |
| **Proficient** | 70–89% | All requirements met with sound choices and diagnostics; minor gaps in the power analysis or the margin argument. |
| **Developing** | 50–69% | Fits correct, but intervals unchecked, the threshold arbitrary, incomplete years included without analysis, or "no significant trend, so stationary". |
| **Beginning** | 0–49% | Sign convention wrong, return periods misunderstood, or a single number without uncertainty. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** `AI_USE_AND_SOURCES.md` is present and consistent with what the defence shows.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns the analysis from a clean environment and gets the reported numbers.
3. **Defence:** the member derives the GEV return level and handles the unseen modification with sound reasoning.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Rainfall file modified (checksum mismatch) | Gate fail until corrected |
| Downloaded third-party data committed to the repository | −2 from dimension 6 |
| Report numbers not regenerated by the code | Up to −5 from dimension 6 |
