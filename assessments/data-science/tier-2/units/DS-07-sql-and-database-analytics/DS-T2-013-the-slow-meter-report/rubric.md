# DS-T2-013 Evaluation Rubric (public)

This rubric is public so you know how you will be judged. Assessors also use a confidential marking guide with calibration notes, but it never adds criteria that are not listed here.

## Weighted dimensions

| # | Dimension | Points | What the assessor looks for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem understanding and technical reasoning | 10 | Explains why each baseline is quadratic-ish and what ordering/tie/frame semantics the spec implies. |
| 2 | Technical correctness | 25 | Identical rows on public and hidden data; work ratios met; empty-day and tie cases correct. |
| 3 | Implementation quality | 10 | Clean CTEs and aliases; no needless nesting; indexes minimal and purposeful. |
| 4 | Methodology and experimentation | 20 | Scaling experiment is fair (same seeds per size, baseline and rewrite measured the same way); alternatives compared for at least two queries. |
| 5 | Testing and evaluation | 10 | Own equivalence checks beyond the provided tests, including a deliberate tie/empty-day case. |
| 6 | Documentation and reproducibility | 10 | Scaling script reruns exactly; plans quoted are regenerable. |
| 7 | Critical thinking and limitations | 10 | States what a VM-step proxy misses, the write cost of indexes, and when the rewrite fails. |
| 8 | Presentation and communication | 5 | Report is concise and its table and plans are readable. |
| | **Total** | **100** | |

## Performance levels

| Level | Share of points | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| **Exemplary** | 90-100% | Exceeds the requirement with insight, for example by quantifying uncertainty or testing an assumption nobody asked about. |
| **Proficient** | 70-89% | Fully meets the requirement with minor gaps that do not affect trustworthiness. |
| **Developing** | 50-69% | Partly meets the requirement; some claims are unjustified or untested. |
| **Beginning** | 0-49% | Missing, incorrect or unexplained. |

## Gates (pass/fail, independent of points)

1. **Integrity:** `AI_USE_AND_SOURCES.md` is present and consistent with what the defence shows.
2. **Reproducibility:** the assessor reruns your work from a clean environment and obtains the submitted results.
3. **Defence:** you trace assessor-chosen results and handle an unseen modification with sound reasoning.
4. **Privacy and ethics:** no real personal data is introduced; any limits stated in §D are respected.

## Automatic deductions

| Issue | Deduction |
|---|---|
| A query special-cases dates, IDs or feeders | Up to -10 from dimension 2 |
| Scaling numbers not regenerated by the submitted script | Up to -5 from dimension 6 |
| Modified the database or the baselines | Gate fail until corrected |
